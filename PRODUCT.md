# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Licensed Canadian amateur radio operators — typically 40s-70s, technically sophisticated, comfortable with precision. They know what a CTCSS tone is and what RBR-4 means. They want to quickly verify information in the field: band allocations, repeater offsets, current propagation conditions.

## Product Purpose
A landing page driving App Store downloads of the Amateur Radio Canada iOS app. Success means a licensed operator finds the page, understands the tool at a glance, and downloads it.

## Brand Personality
Practical, technical, Canadian. Utility-first, no-frills, accurate. Like the hobby itself: not trendy, not flashy. The orange accent carries the app's own identity colour.

## Anti-references
- Glossy consumer-startup pages (Calm, Headspace) — this is a precision utility, not a lifestyle product
- Trendy editorial-magazine aesthetics with italic serif drop caps and broadsheet grids
- Generic SaaS landing pages with abstract gradient blobs and floating feature cards
- Government-portal flatness that feels bureaucratic

## Design Principles
1. Show the tool, not the idea — screenshots do more work than adjectives
2. Speak peer-to-peer — the user is licensed; copy treats them as a peer, not a prospect
3. Canadian context is identity — RAC, ISED, RBR-4 references differentiate this app from generic alternatives
4. Earned precision — every layout decision and data point should feel deliberate
5. Utility-first motion — animation serves orientation, not decoration

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Older user base likely; all body text must pass 4.5:1 contrast. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
